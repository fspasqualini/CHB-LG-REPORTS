Synthesis Note

Canonical Disk Mass-Injecting Overall Findings

This page consolidates what the current fixed-mesh simple-disk mass-injecting studies support after the reboot and follow-up tickets. It is intentionally interpretive rather than exhaustive: use it to see which direct-start lanes are currently readable, which source channels are pathological, and what we are willing to carry forward as the present modeling surface.

Main Conclusion

Continuation is useful as a branch map, not as production biology. The current robust direct-start candidate is pf-only. The coupled pf+theta lane is still branch-sensitive at high source, and theta-only is the clearly pathological channel in the current strong-source screens.

Comfort Zone

We are currently comfortable modeling fixed-mesh, direct-start, simple-disk pf-only runs with conservative early-time dt = 5e-4, steering disabled or weakened, and min_J history used as the primary mechanical admissibility screen.

Not Yet Supported

We are not yet claiming biological-unit calibration, AMR-backed production results, a settled topology-steering controller, or strong coupled pf+theta direct-start biology at high source.

Evidence Surface

  • Direct vs continuation
  • S30 steering control
  • S75 hydraulic sweep
  • Source-channel ablation
  • S75 hydraulic-by-channel follow-up
  • Source-region localization

Direct-Start vs Continuation

The moderate strong-source cold-start lane at s=30 is not a hard collapse, but it is not the same branch as continuation. The direct start shows an early min_J_min ≈ 0.8355 at t ≈ 0.024 before recovering to min_J_final ≈ 1.0374. The matched continuation branch stays near min_J ≈ 1.0473 throughout.

Numerical-Control Pathology at S30

With conservative dt = 5e-4, both steering-on and steering-off direct-start s=30 runs finish cleanly and remain green. Their worst min_J transients are similar (0.8288 vs 0.8386), which supports the narrower interpretation that the original problem is a numerical-control pathology, not purely a steering problem.

Hard Coupled Pathology at S75

The original direct-start coupled s=75 lane remains scientifically uninterpretable. It is red, incomplete, with min_J_min ≈ 0.0135, max_pf_max ≈ 2.36, and 61 failed attempts. Pressure anchoring improves the coupled lane materially, but the pressure-anchored coupled run is still red with min_J_min ≈ 0.2119.

What We Feel Comfortable Modeling

  • Fixed-mesh simple-disk direct-start scans.
  • pf-only source driving with conservative early-time dt.
  • Readable coarsening-like redistribution and deformation trends without sustained mechanical collapse.
  • Moderate source screens where min_J dips early but recovers cleanly, rather than running toward singularity.
  • Stable trend reading from bounded max_pf, modest volume/area proxy growth, and zero or near-zero failed-attempt counts.

Concretely, the pf-only, direct-start, s=30, steering-off localization screen is the current clean reference lane: all six runs finish, all are green, worst min_J stays in the 0.8345–0.8371 range before recovery, peak max_pf remains near 1.038, and the total-volume proxy ends in the modest, stable 1.168–1.170 range.

Source Channels in the Current Implementation

As implemented, the two source knobs act on different equations. s_pf enters the phase-field residual directly, while s_theta enters the water-retention/storage residual directly. In the runtime builder, both are pressure-scaled source terms, optionally masked by region; in the model residual, the corresponding term is added as -dt * source_pf * eta_pf or -dt * source_theta * eta_theta.

  • pf-only: injects directly through the CH phase evolution. The pressure/storage/hydraulic variables still respond, but they do so indirectly through the coupled closure rather than through an explicit theta source. This is the cleanest current modality.
  • theta-only: injects directly through water retention/storage. Any phase/topology change is then indirect, arriving through the coupled mechanics, chemical potential, and transport response. In the high-stress screens, this is the pathological channel.
  • pf+theta: drives both residuals at once using the same pressure-scaled source structure. In theory this represents a genuinely coupled process where phase allocation and hydraulic/storage uptake are both sourced directly. In practice, on the current simple-disk reboot surface, it is the easiest way to overdrive the system and blur whether one is adding phase content, water content, or both.

Why P F-Only Works Better

The strong evidence is the s=75 hydraulic-by-channel follow-up. All three pf-only hydraulic modes are green, complete, and mechanically clean, with worst min_J in the narrow 0.8421–0.8430 range early on and final min_J near 1.044–1.048.

What Theta-Only and P F+Theta Are Good For

They are still useful as diagnostic modalities. They tell us whether the pathology is primarily a hydraulic/storage load, a direct phase load, or their combination. Without them, we would not know that the current strong-source failure is concentrated in the theta channel rather than in pf-only.

What We Lose If We Carry Only P F-Only Forward

We lose mechanistic flexibility. A pure pf-only lane may be too restrictive if the real biology injects material chiefly through storage/water retention or through a truly coupled phase-plus-hydration process. But carrying only pf-only for now does not mean the other modalities are useless; it means they remain diagnostic rather than production-grade until a cleaner physical interpretation is established.

Bottom Line

The current reboot evidence supports a narrow but useful direct-start modeling surface: fixed-mesh simple-disk pf-only runs with conservative early-time stepping, steering disabled or weakened, and min_J history used as the main interpretability screen. The other source modalities remain essential diagnostic controls, but they are not yet the lanes we should present as the stable production biology.
