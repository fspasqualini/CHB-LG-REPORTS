Study Framing

What we tested, why we tested it, and how this page is set up

This study exists because the public Lean surface is SNES-first, but some internal V&V lanes still relied on Newton or behaved differently when swapped to SNES. The practical question was whether that mismatch reflected a real nonlinear-solver difference or a smaller problem in backend activation, option plumbing, or linear-solver defaults.

To isolate that, the investigation fixed the physics and changed one solver dimension at a time. It used a short four-seed disk probe for fast separation, a controlled coupled-strip probe for monitored residual behavior, then a longer public four-seed disk run and a growth-only four-seed confirmation run to check whether the same solver family stayed reliable on lanes closer to the simulations collaborators actually care about.

Headline Findings

What the current evidence supports

  • PETSc SNES with a non-LU Biot stack remains the only family that survives every coupled probe in this investigation.
  • LU is the clearest cross-probe failure ingredient in the Biot stack.
  • All tested Newton variants on the short four-seed disk probe failed to advance, including higher max-iteration and damped variants.
  • The short four-seed disk probe is stricter than the controlled coupled-strip probe: `snes_biot_preonly_ilu` passes on the strip probe but still fails on the short-disk run.
  • The fastest passing case in the short-disk non-LU sweep is `snes_biot_gmres_ilu_restart50` at about 30.07 s total.
Current Decision

Why the current study points to this SNES family

The repo keeps the existing PETSc SNES line-search baseline as the standard coupled solver profile: `snes_type=newtonls`, `snes_linesearch_type=bt`, `snes_max_it=40`. The investigation confirmed that this baseline survives the short-disk probe, the controlled strip probe, the longer public disk run, and the growth-only confirmation run. Explicit non-LU Biot iterative variants such as `gmres + ilu (+ restart50)` are also robust and can be slightly faster, but the timing margin is small enough that continuity, easier reuse of existing simulation packages, and generalizability still favor keeping the established line-search baseline as the repo default.
