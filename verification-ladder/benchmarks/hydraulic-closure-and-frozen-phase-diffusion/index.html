What This Benchmark Verifies

This benchmark combines exact hydrostatic closure with a frozen-phase diffusion mode so the hydraulic sector can be checked without CH or growth interference.

Benchmark Narrative

Narrative

Physical Problem

The hydraulic sector is split into two exact subproblems: a hydrostatic closure state with constant storage/pressure and a frozen-phase cosine diffusion mode.

Narrative

Target Definition

Hydrostatic closure should report zero ranges and zero closure residual, while the diffusion case should match the exact one-step amplitude decay.

Narrative

Why These Observables

The strip schematic explains the setup, the closure-range comparison shows the raw reported and directly recomputed hydrostatic diagnostics, the amplitude plot shows the extracted diffusion observable, and the refinement plot summarizes diffusion accuracy.

Narrative

How To Read The Error

Non-zero hydrostatic ranges indicate a closure/reporting problem. Diffusion error that does not improve under refinement points to hydraulic time stepping or coupling drift.

Narrative

Reference Qualification

Both targets are computed in the harness itself from exact hydrostatic relations and the closed-form one-step diffusion amplitude.

Narrative

Limitations

This page isolates hydraulic closure and frozen-phase diffusion only. It does not exercise the fully coupled mechanics benchmark family.
