Benchmark Narrative

Narrative

Physical Problem

This foundation page checks that the public verification entry surface itself is present: pytest modules, benchmark references, and the named V&V touchpoints that later pages assume already exist.

Narrative

Target Definition

The target is exact pass/fail contract compliance for the public V&V surface described by `tests/vv/test_vv_contract_surface.py`.

Narrative

Why These Observables

The abstract schematic explains which contract layers are being checked, the raw pytest status plot shows individual cases, and the grouped category view summarizes the public V&V surface by responsibility.

Narrative

How To Read The Error

Failures here usually mean a missing benchmark surface, bad manifest glue, or a broken public-facing V&V entry point rather than a numerical issue.

Narrative

Reference Qualification

This benchmark is tied directly to the pytest contract module that defines the public verification-study surface.

Narrative

Limitations

It is an exact contract page, not a numerical benchmark. It only verifies that the expected V&V surface exists and is internally coherent.
