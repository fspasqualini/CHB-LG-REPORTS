Reference Disk Source-Driven Growth: Direct Start

This page consolidates the direct-start investigation surface. It records the screened source modalities, the branch-selection evidence, the numerical-control findings, and the reason we are moving forward with pf-only source-driven forcing under direct-start implementation with conservative early-time dt.

The successful growth reference remains the canonical disk kinematic-growth campaign. This direct-start study explains how the source-driven reboot was screened against that reference and why the forward source law is now limited to pf-only.

Decision

Keep direct start as the biological lane. Use pf-only as the production source modality. Use conservative early-time dt = 5e-4 when opening the onset transient. Treat continuation as reference-only.

Why

The current evidence says the moderate coupled lane is mainly a numerical-control pathology, the strong coupled lane remains pathological, and the robust strong-source candidate is pf-only, not theta-only and not coupled pf+theta.

Current Comfort Zone

Fixed-mesh simple-disk direct-start scans with bounded max_pf, recoverable min_J histories, zero or low failed-attempt counts, and steering disabled or weakened for production-facing interpretation.

How To Read the Modalities

  • pf-only: injects directly through the CH phase evolution. The hydraulic/storage fields respond indirectly through the coupled closure. This is the cleanest current lane and the one we are carrying forward.
  • theta-only: injects directly through storage and water retention. The CH field then carries that disturbance into topology and morphology indirectly. In the strong-source screens, this is the pathological lane.
  • pf+theta: drives both residuals at once. In theory this represents a dual-channel process where phase allocation and storage uptake are both sourced directly. In practice it is the easiest current way to overdrive the system and blur whether we are adding phase content, water content, or both.

What we lose by carrying only pf-only forward is mechanistic flexibility: we give up, for now, any claim that the biology is primarily storage-driven or truly dual-channel from the start. What we gain is a direct-start lane that is currently interpretable without obvious double counting or strong-pathology artifacts.

What Was Tested

  • Direct vs continuation branch comparison
  • Threshold sensitivity and steering sensitivity
  • Hydraulic-mode sweep at strong coupled source
  • Source-channel ablation: pf-only, theta-only, pf+theta
  • Hydraulic-by-channel follow-up at s=75
  • Source-region localization for the chosen pf-only lane

Main Direct-Start Findings

  • The original coupled s=30 direct-start run is ugly but recoverable; smaller initial dt makes it readable.
  • The coupled s=75 direct-start lane remains pathological even when hydraulics help.
  • pf-only stays green across the high-stress hydraulic-by-channel follow-up.
  • theta-only is the failing channel at high source.
  • For the chosen moderate-stress lane, source-region ranking is all > tissue > lumen.
