Kinematic Growth Comparison

This page is the collaborator-facing bridge between the successful mass-preserving kinematic-growth study and the interrupted mass-injecting growth campaign. The point is not that the source-driven study “failed completely”; the point is that the source-driven mechanism can be calibrated into the same broad growth-timescale family while still producing a different robustness and geometric-response story.

Kinematic Reference

The kinematic campaign is the working reference because it reached large final size cleanly while preserving a coherent visual grammar across slow, moderate, and fast growth. The useful timing anchor from that study was the time to approximately double size:

FamilyKinematic rateApprox. time to 2x sizeSource-driven analogue used here
Fastg_rate = 2.5t ≈ 0.440source_rate ≈ 75
Moderateg_rate = 1.0t ≈ 1.098source_rate ≈ 30
Slowg_rate = 0.5t ≈ 2.195source_rate ≈ 15

What Matched

  • The source-rate ladder was chosen so the source-driven mechanism would live on roughly the same slow/moderate/fast time axis as the kinematic study, rather than reaching 8x only through an impulsive source spike.
  • Tissue-only calibration suggested that the source-driven family could be placed in the same broad doubling-time neighborhood as the kinematic family.
  • The retained videos and montages show that source-driven addition does produce large internal rearrangements and strong lumen-family evolution, not just a trivial replay of the kinematic study.

What Diverged

  • The geometric response did not simply mirror the kinematic-growth trajectories even when the mass-growth pace looked comparable.
  • Stability depended strongly on the localization mode. A slow homogeneous case failed, so the failure surface cannot be reduced to “fast source was too large”.
  • Interrupted source-driven runs also exposed an output-path weakness: some partial field files became unreadable, which complicates like-for-like visual comparison unless we improve checkpoint/output robustness.

How To Use This Comparison With A Collaborator

  1. Open the successful kinematic-growth campaign and review the clean slow/moderate/fast progression.
  2. Open the interrupted mass-injecting comparison page and note that the solver/failure pattern depends on source localization.
  3. Use the per-run pages with surviving videos/montages to separate “interesting mechanism behavior” from “artifact loss after failure or interruption”.
  4. Read the source-driven failures as a calibration and stability result, not as a verdict that mass injection is scientifically uninteresting.
