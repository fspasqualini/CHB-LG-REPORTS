Macro Study

Bio-Mass g-Only Calibration WIP

This study is the current public-facing entrypoint for the tissue-growth side of the constituent-addition family. It keeps the canonical disk, fixed mesh, closed hydraulics, and the current tissue-favoring wall term, while varying only the g-growth rate over a fixed horizon.

The goal is to anchor the tissue-growth story before reopening localized-water entry. The same runs also carry the dormant material division-site scaffold metadata that the later seeded theta-only tranche will reuse.

Key packaged surfaces: full rate ladder comparison, regime anchor comparison, and the individual fixed-horizon packages for non-expanding, expanding, and large-volume tissue growth.
